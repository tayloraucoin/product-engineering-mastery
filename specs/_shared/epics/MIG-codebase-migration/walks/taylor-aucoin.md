## Steelman

The runbook's strongest form, the one its author would sign: a repo with two months of unmerged work, no tests, no CI, a hand-written law file and seven decision logs can be brought under the practice in two working days, on one branch, with one commit per step, without a single line of product code changing and without one byte of the host's own records being rewritten. The interview is designed so that nothing is guessed: every prefix, branch, ruling and destination is read from a report or asked for, with the recommended answer first, and the whole table gets one yes before anything is written. Each step ends on a check a cold session can run, so a wrong step is one revert. The hosted world (push, merge, CI, Vercel, Supabase, Stripe) is a wall the thread never crosses: the operator does those with their own hands.
