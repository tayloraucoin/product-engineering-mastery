## How to read this file

- **Decision column.** Text in quotation marks is the report's own words. An ellipsis (…) marks a cut. Text in square brackets is a list of items copied from the report, or my bridge between two quotes.
- **Section column.** Where a report has no numbered sections, the heading name is used.
- **Status.** The report states each decision in its own terms. The mapping to this file's four values is mine:
  - **ruled**: stated as a verdict or ruling.
  - **proposed**: tagged `[PROPOSED]`, or addressed to another role for adoption.
  - **conditional**: carries a stated trigger.
  - **needs call**: names a person who must decide.
  - **superseded by …** and **not adopted**: set when a later ruling (`conflicts.md`, a record) replaces or declines the line. Every changed status keeps its earlier value after "Was:", with the date and the ruling that changed it.
- **Displaces.** Filled only where the report names what is displaced. Otherwise "—".
- **`[product-bound]`.** Names DealReady or Fybr, their people, or their surfaces.
