# P-H — Product operating artifacts

> **Stub — commission before running.** Renumbered from the Toolkit Map's primer 17 (`docs/research/14-toolkit-map-plumb.md`, Appendix) per `conflicts.md` CF-29, with product names generalized. Sharpen the ask against the repo as it stands when you commission it.

**Model:** standard. **Inject:** Compass leads; Envoy and Alembic for the discovery ledger; Gloss for positioning; Plumb captains. **Attach:** `00-shared-context`, those role prompts, `docs/product/*.template.md`, `docs/decisions/decision.template.md`.

**Decision served.** Templates for the product charter, roadmap pins, positioning one-pager, opportunity solution tree and discovery ledger — the product-layer files CF-13 held back until they were researched.

**Ask.**

1. A charter template from the Compass §7 fields, so product facts leave the role prompt.
2. A positioning format chosen from named, dated primary sources.
3. An opportunity solution tree in markdown that links to discovery-ledger observation IDs.
4. The atomic observation schema: verbatim quote, timestamp, source, consent.

**Done criteria.** An agent asked "why did we choose X" answers from `docs/decisions/`; the Compass role's intake shrinks by the facts the charter now holds.

**Not wanted.** Frameworks without a file; PRD templates duplicating the brief (there is none: the brief is the PRD at seed stage); personas unsupported by the ledger.
