# Threads to commission

**What this is.** Stubs for threads the toolkit will need later. Each names its trigger, its role and its decision served, and is filled out when the trigger fires.

**Come here when.** A product reaches the moment the stub names, or you are planning what comes after the current phase.

**Start with.** The stub whose trigger has fired; the triggers are listed in [`../README.md`](../README.md).
