# Measurement

**What this is.** How a product proves something moved. `metrics/` holds events, definitions, experiments and readouts; `evals/` holds the surface, failure-mode and judge templates for an AI feature.

**Come here when.** A brief names a metric, a flag is about to flip, a readout is due, or a product ships an AI surface that makes claims.
